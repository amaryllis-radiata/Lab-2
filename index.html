Miso & White Chocolate Cookies

These decadently baked cookies are a true balance of sweet and savoury. The cookies themselves are fragrant, umami forward ending on a delicious sweet note of creamy white chocolate splashed with hint of flaky sea salt.

Photo by Nele 09/29/2025

This recipe is a modified version from allrecipes @diana71 that I found with a soft cookie base. Just from switching the milk chocolate to white chocolate the flavour of the miso becomes more pronounced leading to an enhanced savouryness

Ingredients

  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tb granulated white sugar
  • 1/2 cup of unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 4 tb shiro miso (white fermented soybean paste)
  • 2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp of Maldon flakey sea salt
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips

Directions

Step 1

Cream brown sugar, butter, and white sugar together in a stand mixer until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Add miso and mix for 30 seconds; scrape down the sides of the bowl. Add egg and vanilla extract; mix for 30 seconds. Add egg yolk; mix until all ingredients are incorporated, scraping down the sides of the bowl as necessary, about 2 minutes.

Step 2

Mix flour, baking powder, and baking soda together in a separate bowl. Add 1 cup flour mixture at a time to the wet ingredients, mixing for 30 to 45 seconds after each addition. Stir in chocolate chips. Turn dough out onto a large sheet of parchment paper. Pull up the sides of the parchment paper and squeeze the batter together into a log. Wrap parchment paper around the log and place in the freezer for 1 hour.

Step 3

Mix flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda together in a separate bowl. Add 1 cup flour mixture at a time to the wet ingredients, mixing for 30 to 45 seconds after each addition. Stir in chocolate chips and walnuts. Turn dough out onto a large sheet of parchment paper. Pull up the sides of the parchment paper and squeeze the batter together into a log. Wrap parchment paper around the log and place in the freezer for 1 hour.

Step 4

Remove cookie dough from the freezer and cut or slice the log into 2 tablespoon-sized pieces. Shape each piece into a round ball and press flat with the palms of your hands to form a somewhat-thick disk, and place 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheet.

Step 5

Bake in batches in the preheated oven until edges are golden brown, 12 to 14 minutes. Remove pan from the oven and allow to sit for 3 minutes before removing cookies to a wire rack to cool completely.
